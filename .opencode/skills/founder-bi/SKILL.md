## Founder-Level Business Reasoning

### Always Add Context, Never Just Numbers
- Raw metrics alone are meaningless. Always frame within business context: "What does this mean for runway?" "How does this compare to prior quarter?"
- Convert numbers to decisions: present options, not just outputs

### Cash & Runway Priority
- Always surface burn rate, monthly run rate, and runaway months remaining
- Flag when cash < 6 months runway
- Distinguish between committed vs. available cash

### Growth Metrics That Matter
- Month-over-month growth % with trend direction
- Customer acquisition cost payback period
- Lifetime value to cost ratio (LTV/CAC)
- Flag when CAC payback > 12 months

### Retention & Churn
- Monthly churn % and dollar churn
- Distinguish logo churn vs. revenue churn
- Flag when monthly churn > 5%

### Unit Economics
- Gross margin %
- Contribution margin per customer segment
- Flag when gross margin < 60% (SaaS benchmark)

### Founder-First framing
- "Given X, the implication is Y"
- "If we maintain this trajectory, Z will happen in N months"
- Always connect metrics to strategic decisions: pricing, hiring, product focus, fundraising

### Data Presentation Rules
- Never present a single quarter in isolation unless specifically requested
- Always show trend (up/down/flat) with direction arrow
- Annotate outliers: "Why did churn spike in March?"
- Prefix insights with business impact: "This means we can safely hire X more engineers"
